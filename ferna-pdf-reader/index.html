Designed for students, researchers and anyone who spends their day with PDFs.

Reading

Every page fitted to your window

Portrait or landscape, each page fills the width of the window, so mixed documents are easy to read.

  • Fit width, fit page or any zoom percentage
  • Rotate pages from the toolbar or a small menu on the page
  • Outline, thumbnails and a clean toolbar that stays out of the way
New in 1.4

Smooth, Xodo-style text selection

Selection snaps to whole words, even in two-column LaTeX papers. A quick popup lets you highlight, underline, copy or ask the AI about the selected text in one tap.

New in 1.4 · සිංහල

Sinhala PDFs, finally readable

Many PDF viewers show Sinhala text as garbled characters. Ferna PDF Reader repairs it, so you can select, copy, search and use the AI in Sinhala.

Private AI

Summaries, questions and quizzes

An AI assistant that runs on your own computer, or on a local server you choose. Your documents are never sent to the cloud.

  • Summaries with page references
  • Ask questions about the document
  • Study quizzes, in English and Sinhala
Annotate

Highlight, draw and write

Highlights, underline, strike-out, area highlight, pen, marker, notes, text boxes and shapes. The tool panel hides itself once you pick a tool. Annotations are saved as standard PDF annotations that open in every PDF reader.

Save helper for Windows (optional)

Lets Save (Ctrl+S) overwrite PDFs opened from your disk, with no Save window.

Download save helper
  1. Unzip it to any folder.
  2. Double-click Install-Save-Helper.bat.
  3. Restart your browser.

Tip: to open PDFs stored on your computer in Chrome, open Extensions → Ferna PDF Reader → Details and turn on Allow access to file URLs.

Ferna PDF Reader – Privacy Policy

Ferna PDF Reader does not collect, store or transmit any personal data or usage data. PDFs and annotations are processed entirely on your computer.

The extension connects to the internet only to:

  • fetch the PDF you open;
  • download an AI model once, if you choose to use the on-device AI (from huggingface.co / raw.githubusercontent.com);
  • reach an AI server address that you enter yourself.

Settings are stored locally in your browser. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
