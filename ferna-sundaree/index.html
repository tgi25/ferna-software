Everything you need, built in

Designed for students, teachers, researchers and families, with the tools other browsers leave to extensions.

Ferna Tabs

Workspaces that keep your tabs in order

Keep Study, Work and Personal apart. Each workspace has its own tabs, pinned sites and colour, and sites you open go into the right workspace by themselves.

  • Common bar for favourites you need everywhere
  • Tab bar on top, left or right, or hidden for more room
  • Separate logins per workspace: two accounts of the same site side by side
Find anything

Never lose a tab again

Tab Overview shows live previews of every open page, grouped by workspace. The Ferna Tabs list opens right at your mouse pointer, and the command palette searches tabs, bookmarks, history and commands at once.

  • Split view: two pages side by side
  • Sleeping tabs save memory and battery
  • A task manager shows what each tab uses
Ferna PDF Reader · built in

Read and annotate PDFs without another app

Every PDF opens in the built-in Ferna PDF Reader: pages fitted to the window, highlights, pen and stylus ink, notes, Sinhala text that copies correctly, and Ctrl+S saves straight into the file.

Ferna Screenshots · built in

Capture a whole page in one picture

Visible area, the entire page, a region or a single element. Then crop, add arrows, text and numbered steps, blur personal details, and save as PNG, JPG or PDF.

Safety

Warns you before you fall for a fake

Ferna Sundaree spots addresses that imitate well-known sites, Sri Lankan banks and your own bookmarks, and warns you before known phishing and malware sites open. The lists are checked on your computer, so no address you visit is sent anywhere.

For families

Parental controls with a parent password

Block adult sites, and optionally gambling and social media, on every page and in every window. SafeSearch and YouTube Restricted Mode are enforced. A child can ask a parent to allow a blocked site once or always.

For teachers

Secure exam mode for online quizzes

Turn the browser into a locked, full-screen exam window that opens only the quiz and the sites you allow. There are no tabs, menus or downloads, leaving the window is undone and logged, and the exam ends only with the invigilator's password or when the time runs out. Give students a .fernaexam file to double-click.

Read & learn

Reading mode and a private AI assistant

Reading mode shows only the article; read aloud speaks it. The AI assistant summarises pages, compares tabs and makes quizzes, and it can run entirely on your own computer with Ollama or LM Studio.

Questions

Is Ferna Sundaree really free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT licence, with no ads, no account and no paid tier. If you find it useful, a coffee helps keep it going.

Will my websites work?

Yes. Ferna Sundaree uses Chromium, the same engine as Chrome and Edge. If a site misbehaves, click the shield and turn blocking off for that site.

Why does Windows say “Unknown publisher”?

The installers are not yet signed with a paid code-signing certificate, so Windows SmartScreen does not recognise the publisher. Check the download against SHA256SUMS.txt, then click More info → Run anyway. After installation, every update is verified with the Ferna signing key.

How is it kept up to date?

Chrome receives security fixes about once a week. They are built into a new Ferna Sundaree release, tested and signed automatically, and your browser installs it by itself.

Can I use my Chrome extensions and bookmarks?

Yes. Add extensions from the Chrome Web Store, and import bookmarks and passwords from Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and most password managers.

Does Netflix work?

Not in this edition. Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player need Widevine copy protection, which this edition does not include. YouTube and most other video sites work normally.

Can schools use it for exams?

Yes. Secure exam mode locks the computer's browser to the quiz and the sites you allow, and only the invigilator's password ends it. Install the MSI on the exam PCs and give students a .fernaexam file. Remove screenshot programs from Linux exam computers, because Linux cannot hide a window from them.

Ferna Sundaree – Privacy

Ferna Sundaree has no telemetry, no analytics and no account. Your history, bookmarks, passwords and settings stay on your computer, encrypted.

Apart from the sites you visit, the browser connects to the internet only to:

  • download ad-blocking, phishing and malware lists (from their publishers) and, if you use parental controls, the parental block lists;
  • check for updates on GitHub and the Ferna update mirror;
  • services you choose to use: translation, an AI provider you set up, the optional leaked-password check (only a short part of a scrambled form of the password is sent), sync through your own cloud folder, and the Chrome Web Store for extensions.
